# Sources for the disease knowledge file

The user's source list (15 Nov 2026), grouped as given. Status after reading on 15 Nov 2026:
**used** = facts taken into `diseases.yaml`; **read, no usable facts** = page read but it gave nothing for the
fields we use; **blocked** = the site refused automated reading (403, bot check or unreadable PDF);
**not read** = skipped to meet the demo deadline. Only *used* sources back a factor.

The sources are mostly about the United States, so region levels exist mainly for US states and a few
countries. Everything else is "unknown" (neutral, ×1.0).

## Used

- https://cropprotectionnetwork.org/encyclopedia/common-rust-of-corn (used: common rust symptoms, night temps 65-70°F, first pustules late June)
- https://content.ces.ncsu.edu/corn-rusts-common-and-southern-rust (used: common rust 16-25°C + 6 h dew, throughout US and southern Canada; southern rust 25-31°C + 4 h dew, primarily southern US, later in season)
- https://cropwatch.unl.edu/plant-disease/corn/southern-rust/ (used: southern rust 80-90°F, high humidity, arrives in Nebraska early to mid July, common in the southeastern US)
- https://lgpress.clemson.edu/journal/common-and-southern-rust-in-field-corn/ (used: common rust April-June and southern rust July-September in South Carolina; temperatures; telling them apart)
- https://cropprotectionnetwork.org/maps/southern-corn-rust (used: southern rust timing by crop stage, tasseling onward)
- https://cropprotectionnetwork.org/encyclopedia/northern-corn-leaf-blight-of-corn (used: NCLB 64-81°F, more than 6 h leaf wetness)
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_corn_leaf_blight (used: 18-27°C wet periods, Corn Belt and Ontario, mid-altitude tropics of Africa, Latin America, China, India; look-alikes Stewart's and Goss's wilt)
- https://www.extension.purdue.edu/extmedia/bp/bp-84-w.pdf (used: NCLB 64-81°F, 6-18 h leaf wetness, late season in Indiana)
- https://cropprotectionnetwork.org/encyclopedia/southern-corn-leaf-blight-of-corn (used: SCLB pathogen, races O and T, symptoms)
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_corn_leaf_blight (used: 68-90°F with high humidity, worldwide, 1970 US epidemic, race C only in China)
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corn_grey_leaf_spot (used: two pathogens, 24-35°C, 12 h leaf wetness, US Midwest and Mid-Atlantic states, Mexico, China, India, Philippines and other regions)
- https://cals.cornell.edu/field-crops/corn/diseases-of-corn/northern-corn-leaf-spot (used: NLS pathogen, symptoms at silking or maturity, warm humid weather, New York)
- https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/crop-production/corn/eyespot-on-corn (used: eyespot pathogen, symptoms, cool wet weather, from V3/4, northern Midwest)
- https://cals.cornell.edu/field-crops/corn/diseases-of-corn/eyespot (used: cool wet weather, found across New York)
- https://publications.mgcafe.uky.edu/sites/publications.ca.uky.edu/files/PPA-51-Curvularia-Leaf-Spot-of-Corn--Remediated.pdf (used: Curvularia leaf spot first US reports Louisiana 2017 and Kentucky 2018, important in China, warm humid, any growth stage; eyespot prefers ~70°F, common in Michigan, Minnesota, Wisconsin, very rare in Kentucky)
- https://cropwatch.unl.edu/plant-disease/corn/physoderma-brown-spot/ (used: Physoderma symptoms, water in the whorl, minor and infrequent in Nebraska and the Midwest)
- https://cropprotectionnetwork.org/encyclopedia/physoderma-brown-spot-of-corn (used: light, free water and 75-85°F)
- https://www.michiganfarmnews.com/about/news-media/msu-seeks-producer-assistance-emerging-corn-leaf-spot-disease-investigation (used: Phaeosphaeria-like leaf spot emerging in 16 Michigan counties, similar reports from New York and Pennsylvania, 2026)
- https://agcrops.osu.edu/specialization-areas/corn/how-climate-affects-corn-production (used: healthy corn temperature and water needs)
